Pav Bhaji

A classic Mumbai street food favorite consisting of a spicy, buttery mashed vegetable gravy served hot alongside toasted, fluffy buttered bread rolls.<\p>

Tools Needed

  • Potato masher
  • large deep skillet or pan
  • pressure cooker
  • chef's knife
  • cutting board.

Ingredients

  1. 3 medium potatoes (boiled)
  2. 1 cup cauliflower florets
  3. 1/2 cup green peas
  4. 2 finely chopped onions
  5. 3 finely chopped tomatoes
  6. 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  7. 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala
  8. 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  9. 4 tbsp butter
  10. salt to taste
  11. fresh coriander
  12. lemon wedges
  13. 6 pav rolls.

How To Prepare

Servings and Info

Yields 4 servings. Total prep and cook time is approximately 40 minutes.

Pav Bhaji

A classic Mumbai street food favorite consisting of a spicy, buttery mashed vegetable gravy served hot alongside toasted, fluffy buttered bread rolls.

Tools Needed

  • Potato masher
  • large deep skillet or pan
  • pressure cooker
  • chef's knife
  • cutting board.

Ingredients

  1. 3 medium potatoes (boiled)
  2. 1 cup cauliflower florets
  3. 1/2 cup green peas
  4. 2 finely chopped onions
  5. 3 finely chopped tomatoes
  6. 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  7. 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala
  8. 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  9. 4 tbsp butter
  10. salt to taste
  11. fresh coriander
  12. lemon wedges
  13. 6 pav rolls.

Ingredient Substitutions

  • Pav Rolls: Slider buns, hamburger buns, or white sandwich bread.
  • Vegetable Additions: Boiled carrots, capsicum (bell pepper), or grated beetroot for a vibrant red color without artificial food coloring.

Timing Breakdown & Servings

  • Servings: 4 servings
  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 25 minutes
  • Total Time: 40 minutes

    How To Prepare

    Servings and Info

    Yields 4 servings. Total prep and cook time is approximately 40 minutes.

    1. Boil potatoes, cauliflower, and green peas in a pressure cooker until completely soft.
    2. Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a pan over medium heat, add chopped onions, and sauté until translucent.
    3. Stir in ginger-garlic paste and chopped tomatoes; cook until tomatoes turn soft and mushy.
    4. Add pav bhaji masala, red chili powder, and salt; stir well to blend spices.
    5. Add the boiled vegetables into the pan along with a splash of water, then mash everything thoroughly using a potato masher until smooth.
    6. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Finish with a tablespoon of butter and fresh coriander.
    7. Slice pav rolls horizontally, toast them on a hot griddle with butter, and serve hot alongside the bhaji, diced onions, and lemon.

    Tip: For an authentic restaurant-style flavor, add a pinch of dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) while mashing the vegetables.

    Recipe Variations

    • Cheese Pav Bhaji: Top the hot bhaji with a generous layer of grated processed Amul cheese before serving.
    • Jain Pav Bhaji: Omit onions and garlic; substitute raw green bananas for potatoes.
    • Paneer Pav Bhaji: Fold grated or cubed paneer (Indian cottage cheese) into the bhaji during the final simmer.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Under-boiling the vegetables (makes mashing difficult).
    • Skimping on butter (affects texture and street-style flavor).
    • And over-diluting the gravy with too much water.

    Congratulations! You've successfully prepared an authentic plate of Pav Bhaji! Enjoy your spicy, buttery dish while it's fresh and steaming hot.

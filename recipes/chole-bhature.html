Chole Bhature

A popular North Indian dish featuring rich, tangy chickpea curry cooked with aromatic spices, paired with deep-fried, fluffy bread.

Tools Needed

  • Rolling pin
  • Deep frying pan (kadhai)
  • Heavy-bottomed pot
  • Mixing bowl
  • Slotted spoon

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups dried chickpeas (soaked overnight)
  • 2 black tea bags
  • 2 onions (puréed)
  • 2 tomatoes (puréed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp chole masala
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for deep frying

Ingredient Substitutions

  • Chickpeas: Canned chickpeas (skip pressure cooking, but simmer longer to absorb flavors).
  • Maida: Whole wheat flour (atta) for a healthier Puri-style alternative instead of traditional Bhature.

Timing Breakdown & Servings

  • Servings: 4 servings
  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 30 minutes
  • Total Time: 50 minutes

How to Prepare

  1. Pressure cook soaked chickpeas with water, salt, and tea bags until tender (5–6 whistles), then discard the tea bags.
  2. For the dough: Mix flour, yogurt, baking powder, a pinch of salt, and water. Knead into a smooth dough, cover, and rest for 2 hours.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pot, add cumin seeds, onion purée, and ginger-garlic paste; cook until golden brown.
  4. Add tomato purée and chole masala; cook until oil separates from the masala.
  5. Add cooked chickpeas along with their water; simmer on low heat for 20 minutes until gravy thickens.
  6. Divide dough into balls, roll into ovals, and deep-fry in smoking hot oil until they puff up fully into golden bhature.
  7. Serve hot chole with fried bhature, pickled onions, and sliced green chilies.

Tip: Add a pinch of carom seeds (ajwain) to the bhature dough to aid digestion and enhance flavor.

Recipe Variations

  • Peshawari Chole: Add diced potatoes and a pinch of pomegranate powder (anardana) for extra sour tanginess.
  • Paneer Stuffed Bhature: Stuff the dough balls with spiced crumbled paneer before rolling and frying.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Frying bhature in oil that isn't hot enough (prevents puffing and makes them greasy).
  • Skipping the dough resting period.
  • Using un-soaked chickpeas.

Storage & Reheating Instructions

  • Storage: Store leftover chole curry in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Bhature are best consumed fresh.
  • Reheating: Warm chole in a saucepan with a little hot water. Reheat cooked bhature in a dry pan or air fryer for 1 minute.

Dal Makhani Chawal

A classic Punjabi comfort meal featuring whole black lentils slow-cooked with cream, butter, and spices, served alongside fluffy basmati rice.

Tools Needed

  • Pressure cooker
  • Heavy-bottomed pot
  • Fine-mesh strainer
  • Ladle

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole black lentils (urad dal, soaked overnight)
  • 1/4 cup red kidney beans (rajma, soaked overnight)
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1.5 cups long-grain basmati rice

Ingredient Substitutions

  • Heavy Cream: Whole milk, Greek yogurt, or coconut cream (for a subtle twist).
  • Basmati Rice: Any long-grain white rice or brown rice.

Timing Breakdown & Servings

  • Servings: 4 servings
  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 45 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour

How to Prepare

  1. Pressure cook soaked black lentils and kidney beans with salt and water for 8–10 whistles until soft. Mash lightly with the back of a spoon.
  2. Rinse basmati rice, cook in 3 cups of water until fluffy, drain excess water, and set aside.
  3. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a large pot, add ginger-garlic paste and tomato purée; cook for 8 minutes until raw smell disappears.
  4. Add red chili powder, garam masala, and the boiled lentils along with cooking liquid.
  5. Simmer dal on low heat for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally and adding water as needed to reach a creamy consistency.
  6. Stir in heavy cream and remaining 1 tbsp butter; cook for an additional 5 minutes.
  7. Serve hot dal topped with a swirl of cream alongside warm basmati rice.

Tip: The secret to restaurant-style flavor is low and slow simmering—the longer it simmers, the richer it gets.

Recipe Variations

  • Dhungar (Smoked) Dal Makhani: Place a glowing piece of charcoal in a small bowl inside the pot, drop ghee over it, and cover for 5 minutes to infuse a smoky flavor.
  • Healthy Low-Fat Version: Substitute cream with low-fat yogurt and use minimal butter.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing the slow-cooking process (dal needs low heat to develop creaminess).
  • Not mashing lentils slightly.
  • Under-soaking the kidney beans.

Storage & Reheating Instructions

  • Storage: Dal tastes even better the next day! Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
  • Reheating: Heat on the stove over medium-low heat, adding water to loosen the thick dal.

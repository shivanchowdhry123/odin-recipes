Noodles & Manchurian

An Indo-Chinese street style combo of wok-tossed vegetable Hakka noodles paired with crispy vegetable balls in a savory, spicy gravy.

Tools Needed

  • Large wok
  • Deep frying pan
  • Mixing bowl
  • Colander
  • Chef's knife

Ingredients

  • 200g Hakka noodles
  • 2 cups finely shredded cabbage
  • 1 cup grated carrots
  • 1/2 cup chopped capsicum
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp chili sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 3 green chilies (chopped)
  • Spring onion greens
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Ingredient Substitutions

  • Hakka Noodles: Spaghetti or ramen noodles.
  • Veggie Balls Base: Add finely chopped mushrooms or cauliflower to the cabbage-carrot mixture.

Timing Breakdown & Servings

  • Servings: 3 servings
  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 25 minutes
  • Total Time: 45 minutes

How to Prepare

  1. Boil noodles according to package instructions, drain, rinse with cold water, toss with 1 tsp oil, and set aside.
  2. In a bowl, combine shredded cabbage, carrots, flour, cornstarch, salt, and pepper. Form into tight lemon-sized balls.
  3. Deep-fry vegetable balls in hot oil until crisp and golden brown; drain on paper towels.
  4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan, sauté garlic, green chilies, and capsicum for 1 minute on high heat.
  5. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, vinegar, and 1 cup water mixed with 1 tbsp cornstarch slurry; bring to a boil until thick and glossy.
  6. Add fried vegetable balls into gravy and simmer for 2 minutes.
  7. In a separate wok, high-heat stir-fry remaining veggies, boiled noodles, 1 tbsp soy sauce, salt, and pepper for 3 minutes. Serve noodles hot with Manchurian gravy and spring onions.

Tip: Toss noodles with a drop of oil right after rinsing in cold water to keep them from sticking together.

Recipe Variations

  • Dry Manchurian: Omit the cornstarch water slurry and toss fried balls directly into the stir-fried sauce for an appetizer version.
  • Schezwan Noodles: Replace green chili sauce with Schezwan sauce for extra heat.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overcooking noodles (makes them mushy instead of firm).
  • Frying Manchurian balls in medium-low oil (causes them to absorb too much oil and break apart).
  • Over-thickening sauce with excess cornstarch.

Storage & Reheating Instructions

  • Storage: Store noodles and Manchurian gravy in separate airtight containers in the fridge for up to 2 days.
  • Reheating: Toss noodles in a hot wok or skillet with a splash of water. Microwave or heat the gravy gently on the stove.

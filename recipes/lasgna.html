Lasagna

A rich Italian baked pasta dish made of alternating layers of lasagna sheets, savory vegetable marinara sauce, and melted cheese.

Tools Needed

  • 9x13-inch baking dish
  • Aluminum foil
  • Large saucepan
  • Cheese grater
  • Colander

Ingredients

  • 12 oven-ready lasagna sheets
  • 3 cups marinara sauce
  • 1.5 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup diced bell peppers and spinach
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Ingredient Substitutions

  • Ricotta: Cottage cheese blended smooth or firm tofu crumbled with lemon juice.
  • Oven-Ready Sheets: Standard lasagna noodles (must be pre-boiled in salted water before assembly).

Timing Breakdown & Servings

  • Servings: 6 servings
  • Prep Time: 25 minutes
  • Cook Time: 45 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

How to Prepare

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Sauté minced garlic, bell peppers, and spinach in a skillet for 5 minutes, then mix into marinara sauce with Italian seasoning.
  3. In a bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, half of the parmesan, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Spread a thin layer of sauce over the bottom of your baking dish.
  5. Lay down 3–4 pasta sheets, spread a layer of ricotta mixture, top with vegetable sauce, and sprinkle mozzarella cheese.
  6. Repeat layers until ingredients are used, finishing with a thick top layer of mozzarella and remaining parmesan.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes; remove foil and bake another 20 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden brown. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing.

Tip: Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes after baking so the cheese sets and slices hold their shape cleanly.

Recipe Variations

  • Meat Lovers Lasagna: Add cooked ground beef or Italian sausage to the marinara sauce.
  • White Sauce Lasagna: Replace marinara sauce with a creamy garlic Bechamel sauce and sautéed mushrooms.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Slicing immediately after removing from oven (causes layers to slide apart).
  • Using too little sauce (results in dry pasta).
  • Baking uncovered the entire time (burns the top cheese).

Storage & Reheating Instructions

  • Storage: Cover leftovers tightly or store slices in containers in the fridge for 3–5 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.
  • Reheating: Bake covered slices at 350°F (175°C) for 15–20 minutes, or microwave individual slices for 2 minutes.
